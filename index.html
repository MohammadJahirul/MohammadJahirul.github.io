INSIDE THE BUILD / 01

INDEPENDENT iOS PRODUCT

Expense Packet.

From a stack of receipts to a claim that’s ready to share.

Swift 6SwiftUISwiftDataVision OCRPDFKitStoreKit 2Swift Testing

The problem

Receipts arrive as paper, images, and PDFs. Currency differences, ambiguous dates, and missing details turn a simple claim into manual work.

What I built

A native capture-to-export workflow: import documents, review extracted fields, organize a claim, and generate a numbered PDF with a matching CSV.

Engineering decisions

  • Keep the core independent. ExpensePacketKit separates extraction, money handling, reports, backups, and purchasing from the SwiftUI app in a Swift 6 package.
  • Make uncertainty visible. Vision OCR feeds a receipt parser with confidence-based review. Multi-currency totals remain separate unless the user supplies conversion rates.
  • Protect responsiveness. OCR uses a dedicated queue; asynchronous extraction supports cancellation while the interface stays responsive.
  • Test the important boundaries. Swift Testing coverage includes parsing, packet layout, CSV output, persistence, backups, and purchase gating.

INSIDE THE BUILD / 02

INDEPENDENT iOS PRODUCT

Talk Trial.

Practice the moment before the moment matters.

SwiftUIAVFoundationSpeechCombineSwift ChartsFoundation ModelsStoreKit 2

The problem

Knowing what to say and being able to say it clearly are different skills. Useful practice needs realistic prompts, a way to listen back, and specific feedback.

What I built

A guided speaking practice app with recorded takes, word-timed transcripts, speech analysis, live coaching, and a playback studio.

Engineering decisions

  • Connect sound to words. AVAudioEngine and Speech recognition capture audio and word timings for synchronized playback and delivery analysis.
  • Give feedback in the moment. Filler and pace detection trigger gentle haptic nudges. Charts expose pauses, pacing, and filler clusters after a take.
  • Use intelligence with a fallback. Foundation Models generates contextual follow-ups on supported devices. A deterministic rule-based engine keeps the feature useful when the model is unavailable.
  • Build a complete product. StoreKit 2 subscriptions, entitlement handling, practice history, and custom scenarios support the free and Pro experiences.
